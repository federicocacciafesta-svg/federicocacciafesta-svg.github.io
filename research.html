Mathematics · Research

Research

My research focuses on dispersive partial differential equations.

01𝒟

Dirac operators with critical potentials

Dispersive, Strichartz and smoothing estimates for Dirac equations with Coulomb and Aharonov–Bohm potentials, in both massless and massive regimes.

02∇

Dispersive PDEs on curved spaces

Dispersive and Strichartz estimates on compact, symmetric and asymptotically flat manifolds, alongside Schrödinger and Helmholtz problems with variable coefficients.

03ψ

Nonlinear Dirac dynamics

Global existence, long-time behaviour and growth of Sobolev norms for nonlinear Dirac equations and coupled relativistic systems.

04ℋ

Harmonic analysis

Singular integral estimates with angular integrability, weighted estimates for functions of self-adjoint operators, and related harmonic-analytic techniques.

05μ

Invariant and Gibbs measures

Construction and invariance of measures for Hamiltonian PDE flows, including nonlinear Schrödinger and KdV equations on the real line, and stability in Wasserstein distances.
